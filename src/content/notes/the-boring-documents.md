everyone talks about AI designing molecules. the part of Anthropic's drug development update i liked most was the paperwork.

at Enveda, manufacturing chemists were spending up to 75% of their time on documentation. Claude reviewed 1,061 pages, caught all 11 issues the human reviewers found, and flagged 7 more they missed. review time went from 25+ hours to under 3. one regulatory document took 4 hours instead of about 3 months.

and Mithril Medicine, a one-person company, used Claude to coordinate contracts and experiments with 11 outside labs. idea to promising mouse results in under 18 months.

this is where i see a lot of quick wins. not the flashy science .. the boring documents that eat half the week.
