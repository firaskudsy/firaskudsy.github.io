before anyone reads "86% deferred to the agent" and says stop using AI .. read the rest of the study.

11 analysts chose to work without the agent. 6 of them (55%) made serious methodological mistakes. with the agent it was 3 out of 50 (6%). and the agent group was faster, median 65 min vs 90.

the authors are careful, the two groups had different experience so it's not a clean causal result. but the direction is clear enough.

so it's not use AI or don't use it. it's how the agent works with you. their ideas make sense to me .. agents that argue more than one side, or ask the expert for their view first before pushing their own. and deciding upfront which decisions must stay human.

for me this is the question that matters most .. not where the agent should continue, where it should stop and ask.
