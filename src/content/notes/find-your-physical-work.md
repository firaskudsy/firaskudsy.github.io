computer science is 4.3% of the world's researchers but 32.7% of the Claude Code research sessions in Anthropic's new report. no surprise there.

the more interesting part is their scenarios. if AI automated all the non-physical steps of research, statistics and CS could get around 15-16x faster. biology, chemistry and materials only 3-4x, because roughly 38-41% of their time is physical lab work AI can't do.

it's basically Amdahl's law. the speedup is capped by the part you can't speed up.

same in engineering teams. you can make coding 10x faster, but if approvals, environments, meetings and releases stay the same .. delivery is not 10x. find your "physical work" first.
