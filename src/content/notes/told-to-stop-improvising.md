what i appreciated in Anthropic's vault particles write-up is that they listed Claude's mistakes too.

Claude helped a small team with limited structural biology experience go from protein search to cryo-EM structures in under four months. purification went from about 3 days to about 90 min, and a first structural model came in 34 hours instead of weeks.

but .. it skipped a standard cryo-EM step and produced a flawed map mixed with its mirror image, and the team told it to stop improvising. a protocol it wrote had an additive that interfered with DNA uptake and cost about two weeks.

that's agents in real life. very fast, very useful, and sometimes creative where you don't want creativity. the standard steps need to stay standard.

(vaults btw are hollow protein shells in almost all our cells. found in 1986, and we still don't know what they do)
