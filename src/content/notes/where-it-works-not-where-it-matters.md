Anthropic looked at ~207k research sessions on Claude plus a survey of 528 scientists. one gap stood out to me..

scientists say ideation is the most important part of getting important results (35.6 out of 100 points). but ideation is only 4.2% of Claude sessions. data analysis is 33.7%, writing 32%. drafting manuscripts alone is 18.6%.

so we use AI where it works best today, not where it matters most.

i think software is the same. most AI usage is writing code and docs. very little is "help me decide what to build and why" .. and that's usually where the real value, or the real waste, is.
