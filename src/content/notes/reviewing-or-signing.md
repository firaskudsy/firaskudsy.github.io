another detail from the same Anthropic study on agents and expert judgment..

77% of the analysts reused the agent's arguments in their own explanation, sometimes word for word. only 15% gave the agent any credit.

and they weren't lying. they really believed the conclusion was theirs. none of them suspected the agent's preference was set by a coin flip.

this is the part people miss when they say "don't worry, a human reviews the AI output". the authors put it well .. a reviewer is only an independent check if the judgment is really their own.

if the agent wrote the analysis, picked the method and argued for it .. and i approve it .. am i reviewing or am i signing?
